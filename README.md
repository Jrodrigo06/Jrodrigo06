### Projects

**[Moonseye](https://moonseye.vercel.app)**
A knowledge base for a [Deepwoken](https://www.roblox.com/games/4111023553/Deepwoken) that keeps itself constantly up to date with a Kafka + Go pipeline that watches for new content and feeds a Neo4j graph, vector, and Postgres database
`Python` `Go` `Kafka` `Neo4j` `FastAPI` `pgvector`

**[Unsprawl](https://github.com/GenerateNU/unsprawl)** — *Tech Lead, w/ [Generate](https://generatenu.com/)*
Pulls scattered tasks out of Slack, Trello, and Jira into one place and uses an LLM judge to catch duplicates before they pile up.
`Python` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Gemini`

**[Remetra](https://github.com/GenerateNU/remetra)** — *Tech Lead, w/ [Generate](https://generatenu.com/)*
Helps people with autoimmune conditions figure out which foods actually trigger their symptoms, using real statistics instead of guesswork.
`FastAPI` `React Native` `pgvector` `Docker`

**[GraphDTA-Replication](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16934665)**
Replicated a couple of graph neural net architectures to predict how strongly a drug binds its target. Following [GraphDTA](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btaa921)
`PyTorch` `RDKit`

**[Blackjack RL](https://jerodrigo.com/blackjackrl.pdf)**
Trained a DQN to play blackjack about as well as you realistically can against the house edge.
`Gymnasium` `PyTorch`
